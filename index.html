About

Hanasora is an independent software company developing digital products and applications across a range of projects. We work end to end — from concept and design through engineering, release, and ongoing refinement.

Our work spans consumer and project-based software, united by a single goal: building tools that are considered, dependable, and a pleasure to use.

What we do

  • Design and develop software applications
  • Shape product identities and user experiences
  • Engineer reliable, maintainable systems
  • Support and improve products over time

Approach

Our design language draws on Scandinavian and Japanese traditions: simplicity, restraint, utility, and respect for detail.

We favour software that feels clean and calm — easy to understand, quiet in use, and built to last.

Principles

  • Human-centered product thinking
  • Careful interface design and visual consistency
  • Privacy and trust by default
  • Long-term maintainability and refinement
